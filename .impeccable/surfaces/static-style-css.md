## Direction contract

THESIS: Workbench shows its editing history in the margin — a revision/change-bar system borrowed from engineering-drawing title blocks — refusing the clean-magazine-feature arrangement every technical blog defaults to, where only the final polished answer survives.

OWN-WORLD: Off-white/graphite paper ground (#f5f4f0 bg, #201f1d ink), one rust/amber accent (#c8732a) reserved for revision marks and active state — never decorative. Masthead reads as a drafting title block (sheet / rev / date). Headlines set in Zilla Slab (sturdy, engineering-adjacent slab serif); body in Spectral (calm literary serif, real reading contrast); metadata/rev numbers in JetBrains Mono. A margin column of dated rev-ticks runs beside the reading column on post pages; the post index reads as a sheet list (number, rev, title), not a card grid.

STORY: A reader arrives at an article, immediately understands they're reading one numbered sheet in an ongoing series, and sees — via the margin's rev ticks — that the post itself was corrected after first publishing. That's the "process over results" positioning made visible structurally, not asserted in copy.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A title-block masthead across the top (brand left; sheet / rev / date right, divided by a heavy rule) above a two-column body: the reading column (measure ~620px, Spectral 18–19px/1.7) on the left, a narrow margin rail of dated revision ticks on the right. The post index renders as a sheet list (number, rev, title, date, tag), not a card grid.

FORM: Rev Mark — IMPECCABLE'S PICK card, chosen over the assigned "Card Catalog" direction (Zettelkasten numbering) and the competitive "Construction Grid" challenger (Crouwel gridded specimen); raised by nothing (picked directly, no fusion needed since it topped the model's own ranked list). concept-seed direction round, scope=direction, mode=read. seed:73d81f3aef9ae0f6
